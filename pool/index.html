How these numbers are made

  • The pool writes them every 30 seconds; this page reads them every 30 seconds. They can be a minute old.
  • Hash rates are estimates: the work of the shares accepted in the last ten minutes, divided by ten minutes. A miner that just started, or that is unlucky, shows less than it does.
  • The pool's public key, which the miner pins, is –.
  • Anyone who knows an address can look it up here, as on any public pool.
